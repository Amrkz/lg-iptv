## English (primary)

**Name (≤30):** Kayan IPTV

**Subtitle (≤30):** Player for your own playlists

**Promotional text (≤170):**
A clean, fast player for the M3U and Xtream playlists you already have. Search first, favorites, history, Arabic & English. No channels included, no ads, no tracking.

**Keywords (≤100, comma-separated, no spaces):**
m3u,m3u8,xtream,iptv player,playlist,live tv,hls,stream player,vod,epg,arabic,video player

**Description (≤4000):**
Kayan IPTV is a clean media player for iPhone and iPad. It does NOT include any TV channels, movies, or series — you add your own playlist.

WHAT YOU GET
• Play M3U / M3U8 playlists and Xtream Codes accounts you already have
• Browse Live TV, Movies, and Series from your own provider
• Search right at the front — type and press Enter / Go
• Favorites and Recent history (series grouped together)
• Player controls that stay put while paused or loading
• Seven themes and a full Control Center: playlists & accounts, player & buffering, subtitles & audio, parental PIN, appearance, backup & reset
• English and Arabic with full right-to-left layout

IMPORTANT
Kayan IPTV is a player only. We do not sell, host, or provide any streams or content. You are responsible for using playlists and credentials you are allowed to use. The app connects only to the server you configure. No account, no tracking, no ads.

Need help? amokazes@gmail.com

**What's New (1.0.6):**
First iPhone release. Search is now the first tab and starts on Enter. Player controls no longer disappear.
