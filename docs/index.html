Privacy Policy — Kayan IPTV

Last updated: 6 November 2026 · Developer: Amr Kazzaz (Amrkz) · Contact: amokazes@gmail.com

Kayan IPTV is a media player only. It does not include, host, sell, or provide any TV channels, movies, series, or playlists. You supply your own M3U playlist or Xtream Codes login from a provider you choose.

What we collect

We collect nothing. There are no analytics, crash reporters, advertising SDKs, or trackers. No account with us is required. The developer never receives your playlists, passwords, watch history, or device identifiers from the app.

Data stored on your device

Everything the app remembers stays only on your device: playlist or Xtream credentials you enter, favorites, history, progress, theme, language, parental PIN, player preferences, and optional downloads you start. Uninstalling the app or using Logout / Reset removes this data.

Network use

The app connects only to your configured IPTV provider and to artwork hosts named in that provider’s lists. We do not proxy or log that traffic.

Android permissions

  • Internet — reach your provider and load artwork
  • Network state — detect connectivity
  • Wake lock — keep playback awake while you watch

No SMS, call log, contacts, precise location, microphone, or camera access is requested.

Children

Parental PIN features are local device locks. Do not share adult-oriented playlists with children.
