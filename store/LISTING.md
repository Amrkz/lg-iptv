### Store description
Kayan IPTV is a player for LG webOS TVs. It does not include any TV channels, films, or series. You sign in with an Xtream Codes account or an M3U playlist from a provider you already use.

Live TV, films, and series, with favorites and Continue Watching. The app opens on the section you used last. On live TV, Up and Down change the channel, the number keys jump to a channel, and Green returns to the last channel.

Series can play the next episode, with Skip intro and a pause after three episodes in a row. The player has picture size, audio and subtitle language, subtitle size, and a sleep timer. Catch-up is available on channels where your provider keeps an archive.

English and Arabic. Arabic mirrors the sign-in screen, the top bar, and the dialogs. The rows and the player stay left to right so the remote keys behave the same.

A parental PIN can hide adult category names on this TV. A settings backup does not include your provider password or the PIN.

There is no account with us, no advertising, and no tracking. The app talks only to the server you enter. You are responsible for using a service you are allowed to use.
